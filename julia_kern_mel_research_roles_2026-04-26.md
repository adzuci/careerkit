## Assumptions and filters

- Julia's LinkedIn profile and CV attachment were not accessible from this environment, so fit is based on the Linear issue summary: analytics leadership, modern data platforms, research/evaluation, MEL, and client delivery.
- Primary target: remote US roles, or roles with a meaningful Boston/hybrid angle, in philanthropy, nonprofit, academic, NGO, government-adjacent, or social-impact contexts.
- Compensation target: $120,000+ base for government/commercial roles. For nonprofit/philanthropy roles, I included a few high-fit roles that top out at or near $120,000 because the issue title notes openness to less in those sectors.
- Quality filter: excluded roles that were clearly closed, on-site outside Boston, junior, salary-mismatched, or only weakly connected to research/evaluation/impact.
